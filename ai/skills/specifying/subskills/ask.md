# Ask

Run one question round and write its answers. Every stage asks through here.

When the carbon unit says in plain words to answer the questions without them ("answer them yourself", "no input from me"), ask nothing in any round, the hand off rounds included: close each question on its recommendation as an `assumed` decision whose `why` is the recommendation's reason, take the review gate the carbon unit named or else `gating-reviews`, then cut and build. "Spec it", a whiteboard, and earlier discussion still get question rounds.

1. Ask with your structured question tool (AskUserQuestion in Claude Code); without one, print the round as a numbered list with lettered options and wait for the reply. Ask up to four mutually independent questions, each offering two to four real alternatives with their costs, the recommended one first, marked `(Recommended)`, with its reason. Recommend the option with the best design: a migration, extra files or a larger edit is a cost listed on that option, and the recommendation still goes to the best design. Every question shows its upstream, so the carbon unit sees whether an option changes the source; `guard-workflow` denies a round during /specifying that leaves a question without one and names the forms it accepts.
2. Show what is being chosen. When an option is a type, signature, call graph, pseudocode, flow, or mockup, put it in the option's `preview`, or under the option in a plain-text round, so the alternatives sit side by side.
3. Write every answer into the graph right away. A chosen option or a settling free-text answer becomes a `decided` decision, or the chosen code of a design node, whether it closed a branch or a slice question, so a later challenge that undoes it shows at hand off. A partial answer records what it settled and returns as a narrower branch. A redirecting answer rewrites the goal, the non-goals, and the affected branches first.
