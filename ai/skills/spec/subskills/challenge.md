# Challenge

1. Invoke the `plan-challenge` skill with the output of `render.ts <spec.json> goal` as goal, the output of `render.ts <spec.json> plan` as plan text, and `repoRoot` as repo path. The plan lists the units ready for work; deferred units wait for their open question.
2. Judge the verdicts as `plan-challenge` prescribes. A merge the validator would reject as over the reading budget is rebutted by that failure. Take any verdict that would undo a `decided` decision to the carbon unit as one question quoting the challenger's `because`.
3. Apply the surviving verdicts to the unit nodes and their edges.
4. Set `challengeStamp` to the stamp line, `challengedUnitIds` to the ids of the units ready for work, and status to `challenged`. When every lane dropped out, keep status `sliced` and report the design spec as unstamped.
