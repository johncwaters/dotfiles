# Explainer

A narrated, stepped instructional piece: the scene plus narration subtitles, a step caption, and chapter controls.

- Concept: one explainer covers one idea in about four steps. The beat table runs in story time from 0 to 20 seconds, and each row also names its caption step.
- Scene: chapter buttons jump to each step's start.
- Sound: run every step of [sound](sound.md); an explainer is always narrated.
- Review: the target viewer is the developer the docs serve, and the accuracy owner is a docs lead.
