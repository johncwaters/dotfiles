# Answer back

Walk the core flow one action at a time. Each action puts three answers on screen:

- **What just happened.** A visible result or progress the moment the action lands. A finished action shows its outcome, even a partial one; something beats nothing.
- **How to take it back.** Undo, cancel, edit, or back, reachable from where the carbon unit is standing.
- **What to do next.** One clear next step, named in the copy.

Answers render inline, beside the control or region that caused them, before any overlay. A modal comes last, after inline and progressive options are ruled out.

A design rationale naming a behavior the interface lacks is a gap. Check the rendered screen, not the rationale.
