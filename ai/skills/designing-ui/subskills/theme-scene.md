# Theme scene

Dark or light comes from the scene. Write one sentence of physical scene first: who uses this, where, in what light, in what mood. "Dashboard" forces nothing; "me checking a deploy on a laptop in a dim room at midnight" does.

If someone could guess the palette from the category name alone, rework the scene. A taste that fixes the theme already answered dark or light; say so and go on to the richness below.

Then name where the richness goes:

- **Moments**: one or two places that carry color, material, and detail, picked from what the product shows, like a chart whose colors each mean a series or a rendered object the product deals with. The chrome around them (navigation, buttons, borders, labels) stays quiet in the taste's neutrals and one accent.
- **Signature**: one small detail only this product would have, used the same way everywhere it appears.

Describe the character in concrete words. Clean, modern, sleek, minimal, and premium fit every template, so they decide nothing.
